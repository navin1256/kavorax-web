## 4. Header & Footer Landmark Consolidation Evidence

### Problem Diagnosed
In earlier iterations, `app/components/Header.vue` and `app/components/Footer.vue` were created alongside `app/components/layout/AppHeader.vue` and `app/components/layout/AppFooter.vue`.
- `layouts/default.vue` mounted `<AppHeader />` and `<AppFooter />`.
- Several legacy pages (`contact-us.vue`, `solutions.vue`, `industries.vue`, `careers.vue`, `resources.vue`) explicitly mounted `<Header />` and `<Footer />` inside their own templates.
- This resulted in **two simultaneous headers and footers** being rendered on those pages, accompanied by multiple `<main>` tags.

### Remediation Applied
1. Safely archived untracked legacy pages into `legacy_archive/pages/`.
2. Removed dead untracked `app/components/Header.vue` and `app/components/Footer.vue` from the project.
3. Updated `app/components/layout/AppFooter.vue` to link directly to the newly implemented `/privacy-policy` and `/terms-of-service` routes.
4. Verified that `layouts/default.vue` remains the single, authoritative provider of the global `<AppHeader />`, `<main>`, `<AppFooter />`, `<WhatsAppWidget />`, and `<CookieBanner />`.
