## UI / design work
- Before any UI, design, styling or copy-layout task, read `design-system/kavorax/MASTER.md`.
  Its "KavoraX — Brand Overrides" section takes priority over the ui-ux-pro-max skill's
  generated recommendations and over your own defaults.
- Use the ui-ux-pro-max skill for UX guidelines, accessibility checks and Nuxt/Vue stack
  guidance (`--stack nuxtjs`), not to pick colours, fonts or styles that contradict the brand.
